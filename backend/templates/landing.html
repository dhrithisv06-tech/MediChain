How MediChain Works

A privacy-first pipeline where files are encrypted locally, stored securely off-chain, and anchored cryptographically on Ethereum.

1. Upload & Encrypt

Files are hashed with SHA-256 and encrypted client/server-side using military-grade AES-256-GCM.

2. Blockchain Anchor

The cryptographic hash and metadata are registered on the Solidity Smart Contract. No raw medical data touches the chain.

3. Patient Access Control

Doctors search by Patient ID to request access. Only the patient can approve or revoke access via smart contract calls.

4. Instant Verification

Any party can re-hash the document at any time to verify it matches the immutable blockchain register. Tampering is flagged in seconds.

You own your records. Doctors cannot view or download documents unless you explicitly approve their request. Revoke permissions at any time with a single click.

SHA-256 Tamper Detection

Every document generates a unique 256-bit hash. Even changing a single character or byte results in a completely different hash, immediately flagging forgery.

AES-256-GCM Encryption

Stored files are encrypted at rest with random 96-bit nonces and authenticated tags, ensuring confidential medical scans remain private even if storage is breached.

Solidity Smart Contracts

Security Blueprint

Why MediChain Never Stores Plaintext Health Data on Chain

Public or private blockchains are immutable by design. Storing raw medical documents or patient PII on a blockchain violates patient privacy laws (HIPAA/GDPR). MediChain implements the industry-standard off-chain hybrid model:

Encrypted Off-Chain Storage

Documents are encrypted with unique AES-256-GCM keys. Only the ciphertext is stored outside the blockchain.

On-Chain Cryptographic Registry

The smart contract records the document SHA-256 digest, record ID, and patient ID, providing an indelible public proof of existence.

Smart Contract Permission Guards

Doctor access flags reside inside the Solidity state machine. The backend validates both DB and smart contract before releasing any file.
